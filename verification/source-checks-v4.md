# Primary-source checks for revision 4

The revision checked the feedback's references in the primary papers. Source PDFs used for the check are not redistributed. The manuscript bibliography contains the publication records.

| Source | Verified locator and role |
| --- | --- |
| Zhang–Liu–Chen, arXiv:2609.26362v1 | Main Eq. (6) and Supplement Theorem 1, Eqs. (S12)–(S18): Gaussian limiting optimal LOCC **root fidelity** under a logarithmic-rank hypothesis. The chain paper uses root fidelity `1−E_d/2`; its local-unitary result and fixed-target remainder are distinct additions. |
| Mančinska, arXiv:2610.06512v1 | Eq. (10): uniform 25-pair flagged square; Proposition 1 and Eq. (12): perfect rank-five state; Theorem 3 and Corollary 4: uniform maximally entangled/tracial gap. Restriction to the nine copies already yields a qualitative gap for the linked game. |
| Liang–Vértesi–Brunner, PRA 83, 022108 (2011) | Section III, Theorem 1: finite maximally entangled states cannot attain the maximum for their tilted Clauser–Horne expression. The stronger nonviolation claim is their Conjecture 1 and is not attributed as a theorem. |
| Acín–Massar–Pironio, PRL 108, 100402 (2012) | Eq. (7) and its optimization give the tilted-CHSH benchmark after setting their correlator parameter to one. |
| Bamps–Pironio, PRA 91, 052111 (2015) | Section II.A and Eqs. (3)–(4): optimal two-qubit strategies; Section II.B: sum-of-squares proofs. The chosen four-spin witness need not saturate the unrestricted optimum. |
| Kera et al., arXiv:2509.15749v2 | Section 3 and Theorem 1: one-side Gaussian trace-distance bound `11 d ε^(1/4)` in terms of covariance density; Appendix Corollary A.16 lifts to a global bound in another metric. The entropy-based lower constraint on the density parameter does not supply a matching chain-size upper bound. |
| Sierant, arXiv:2608.28563v2 | Proposition 2 and Supplement Sections S5–S6: smoothed Calabrese–Lefèvre rank-window asymptotics and their LOCC interpretation. These concern a different quantity from squared local-unitary error. |

| Fagotti–Calabrese, J. Stat. Mech. P01017 (2011) | Section 5, Eq. (70): finite-open XX Rényi coefficient and boundary chord, with an ansatz for subleading terms. |
| de Boer–Järvelä–Keski-Vakkuri, PRD 99, 066012 (2019); Arias et al., JHEP 03 (2023) 175 | Capacity is the variance of the entanglement Hamiltonian; its leading CFT coefficient agrees with entropy. The open-boundary geometry is supplied by the boundary references. |
